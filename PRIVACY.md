# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

FocusWall is built to keep your data on your phone.

- **No data collection.** FocusWall does not collect, transmit or share any personal information.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
- **No accounts, ads or analytics.** There is no sign-in, no advertising SDK and no tracking.
- **Local storage only.** Your tasks and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps can't read them.
- **Deleting your data.** Uninstalling FocusWall, or clearing its storage in Settings › Apps › FocusWall, removes all your tasks permanently.
- **Backups.** If Android's device backup is turned on, the system may include FocusWall's data in your personal backup, under your own Google account and backup settings.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
