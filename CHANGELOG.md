## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28
### Added
- Live wallpaper that shows a **"Today's Focus"** card with the top 3 / 5 / 7 open tasks
- Automatic ranking: priority (High > Medium > Low), then nearest due date, then oldest first
- Due-date labels: overdue in red, due today in amber, relative dates for the coming week
- **Tap-to-complete** on the home screen, with a check-and-strike animation
- Three animated backgrounds: Aurora, Particles, Calm
- Card position (Top / Middle / Bottom) and battery-saver mode
- App screen to add, edit and delete tasks, with a live miniature wallpaper preview
- "+N more" and "done today" counters on the wallpaper
- GitHub Actions workflow that builds the APK on every push
